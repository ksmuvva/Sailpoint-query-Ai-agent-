## Instructions

1. **Understand the context**:
   - What industry/regulatory environment? (finance, healthcare, government)
   - What scale? (users, applications, roles)

   - Architecture patterns

3. **Provide strategic guidance** covering:

   - SOD policy design
   - Lifecycle management strategy
   - Compliance mapping
   - Implementation roadmap

## Key Frameworks
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: Identity management controls
- **ISO 27001**: Access control (A.9), identity management
- **SOX Section 404**: Internal controls over financial reporting
- **HIPAA**: Minimum necessary access, audit trails
- **GDPR**: Right to access, data minimization, consent
- **PCI-DSS**: Access control, authentication, monitoring
